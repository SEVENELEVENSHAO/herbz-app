# APK reference archive

These files preserve the formula and herb records extracted from the two APK sources.

- `apk-formulas.json`: formula records and source-native formula taxonomy.
- `apk-herbs.json`: herb records and source-native materia medica taxonomy.

They are intentionally stored outside `src/data` and are not imported by the application. Changes here do not affect the live library or its categories. All extracted medical-reference content remains pending review.
